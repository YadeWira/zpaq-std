BLAKE3 1.8.2 (https://github.com/BLAKE3-team/BLAKE3, tag 1.8.2), from its release's source
archive (sha256 6b51aefe51596978...): only the SIMD implementations of its C library, unmodified:
the assembly for x86-64 (SSE2, SSE4.1, AVX2, AVX-512; the unix and the windows_gnu files) and
blake3_neon.c with the two headers it includes. CC0 1.0, or Apache License 2.0 (with LLVM
exceptions), at the user's choice (the three licence files).

zpaq-std has BLAKE3's portable code and its dispatch inside zpaq-std.cpp (from zpaqfranz); since
2.14.0 the builds for x86-64 (Linux, Windows) and AArch64 (Linux, macOS) link these files, and the
dispatch picks one at run time by what the CPU has. The other builds stay with the portable code.
Do not patch these files here: a new release comes in whole.
