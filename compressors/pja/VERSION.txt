EXPERIMENTAL in zpaq-std, not recommended yet (-sa warns, 00737).
zpaq-std's codecs for specific formats, -sa: PJA's family, one plugin with five
codecs: pjax (JPEG that needs PJA's stream version 6: MPO and Ultra HDR, 12 bits, DNL,
DRI without RST), pja (JPEG), pjall (lossless JPEG), pjals (JPEG-LS) and pjalsx
(JPEG-LS with subsampled components). -sa and -sa:pja use the five, in that order. Free Pascal: by the project's rule, a codec in
Rust or Free Pascal is built only as a plugin (codecs/pja.dll or pja.so, in the extra-codecs
download), never linked into zpaq-std itself. Every block carries the codec's ZPAQL
decoder (pas/plugin/pjazpaql.inc, pjaxzpaql.inc, pjallzpaql.inc, pjalszpaql.inc,
pjalsxzpaql.inc), so any zpaq extracts it without the plugin; those five programs are
frozen under these codec names.
